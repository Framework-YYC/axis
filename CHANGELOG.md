- **Finance corrections (staging for review; migration 0008).** Nothing financial is ever deleted. A transaction can be edited (every change keeps a before/after history row with who and why), **voided** (stays on record with its reason but counts nowhere: no balance, total, bill, reimbursement or net worth) or **reversed** (one offsetting entry; both stay, balances net to zero, and the pair is left out of income, spending and debt totals together so a reversal in another month never shows as negative income). Voided, reversed and reversal rows are labelled in words with a Show voided toggle. A **bill payment can be reversed**: the occurrence is unpaid or overdue again, its transaction is voided or reversed, the task that payment completed is reopened (a task the owner had already reopened is left alone) and the same due date can be paid again once, with no duplicate. **Loan payments split into principal and interest**: principal reduces the loan and is the debt repaid, interest is an expense (it lowers net worth); unsplit payments count as principal and are flagged; an interest estimate from the stated rate is offered only as a labelled button, never saved on its own. **Reimbursements are tracked apart from income and spending**: a claim links to the expense(s) it recovers, deposits are reconciled against it (outstanding, partly received, received, over-received flagged), and each expense shows unclaimed, claimed, partly received or reimbursed. New operations `void_transaction`, `reverse_transaction`, `reverse_bill_payment` (and the same as record kinds for the Financial assistant through the existing inbox request, no new request names); the Financial assistant prompt is v3. The approved Finance dashboard design and every figure for non-corrected data are unchanged.
